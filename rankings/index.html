Data source
Directly from ABA disclosures
Every number here originates from ABA 509 Required Disclosure Reports and ABA Employment Summary Reports — the official, audited filings all accredited law schools submit annually. No US News methodology, no scraped aggregations, no synthetic composites.
Coverage
All 197 ABA-accredited schools
Rankings include every ABA-accredited law school in the United States — not a curated subset. Tier 3 schools receive the same data depth as T14 schools. Filters let you narrow by tier if needed.
Methodology
Defined, not hidden
Each ranking page explains exactly what's being measured, how percentages are computed, and what the denominator is. Bar passage uses first-time takers. Employment uses total graduates. Nothing is left ambiguous.
Updates
Class of 2025 employment · 2024 bar passage
Employment rankings reflect ABA Class of 2025 outcome data. Bar passage reflects first-time taker results from the Class of 2024. Scholarship data uses 5-year enrollment-weighted averages through 2025.
