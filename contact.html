Get in Touch

Contact

Found an error in the data, have a question about the site, or want to flag something that looks off on a school's page? Send it over.

Email
contact@atlaslegis.com

Direct email only — there's no contact form, no support ticket queue, and no chatbot. A person reads every message.

What to write in about
Data corrections
A number that doesn't match a school's ABA 509 report or employment summary. Include the school name and which figure looks wrong — a link to the source document speeds things up.
Bugs
Something broken on the map, a school page that won't load, or a calculation in the debt planner or scholarship estimator that doesn't add up.
Missing schools
An ABA-accredited school you'd expect to see that isn't on the map.
Everything else
Feedback, questions about methodology, or anything that didn't fit the categories above.

Response time: This is a small, independent project, so replies aren't instant — but every email gets read, and corrections to the underlying data get prioritized.
